# Golden Kernel

Create a highly aesthetic, cinematic, futuristic website focused entirely on corn. The experience should feel like a premium product launch page (Apple-style), built around fullscreen immersive video sections.

CORE CONCEPT (VERY IMPORTANT)

Each section MUST use a fullscreen video as the background, covering the entire viewport (edge-to-edge).

Videos should fill 100% width and height of the screen

No boxed layouts

No visible background behind videos

The website is essentially a sequence of fullscreen video scenes

Text and UI should be layered ON TOP of the videos.

VIDEO BACKGROUND BEHAVIOR

Each section = one fullscreen video

Videos must:

autoplay (muted)

loop seamlessly

be optimized for web

maintain aspect ratio while covering entire screen (object-fit: cover)

Use a slight dark/gradient overlay when needed to improve text readability

SCROLL EXPERIENCE (CRITICAL)

Implement cinematic scroll storytelling:

Each video section is sticky fullscreen

As user scrolls:

video can either:

subtly scrub with scroll OR

play slowly in loop with slight speed control

Smooth crossfade transitions between sections

No hard cuts

Scrolling should feel:
fluid, cinematic, controlled, premium

VISUAL SYSTEM

Minimal UI

No heavy containers

Transparent/glass UI elements only

Large bold typography (white or soft cream)

Soft shadows and glow for readability

Colors:

Base: video-driven

Accents: golden yellow, green

Overlays: subtle dark gradients

SECTION FLOW (VIDEO-DRIVEN)

HERO (CORN HEAD VIDEO)

Fullscreen corn video as background

Slow rotation, particles, sun rays

Centered headline:
“CORN. REIMAGINED.”

Subtle fade-in animation

DNA ENERGY VIDEO

Fullscreen glowing DNA helix

Slow rotation, particles

Text appears left or center

Smooth fade-in

PARTICLE DNA VIDEO

DNA made of light dots

Minimal UI

Parallax particles layered over video

SEED VIDEO

Single floating seed

Strong focus center

Minimal text

GROWTH IN POT VIDEO

Seed already planted

Plant grows into young corn plant

Sun rays + particles

Calm, grounded section

FULL PLANT VIDEO

Tall corn plant

Camera tracking vertically

Use scroll illusion (content feels like moving through plant)

FIELD VIDEO (AERIAL)

Massive corn field

Slow camera glide

Sense of scale and завершение

FINAL CTA (OPTIONAL VIDEO OR STATIC)

Can be subtle looping background or faded video

Minimal message:
“A simple grain. A golden world.”

TRANSITIONS

Between sections:

Use smooth fade / crossfade

Slight blur transition optional

Maintain color continuity

INTERACTION DETAILS

Text fades in/out based on scroll position

No aggressive motion

No distracting UI

Hover effects minimal (slight glow or scale)

PERFORMANCE

Use compressed high-quality videos

Lazy load upcoming sections

Ensure mobile responsiveness (fallback images if needed)

GOAL

The website should feel like:
A cinematic, immersive, fullscreen visual experience where each scroll reveals a new chapter — driven entirely by video.

No visible background.
No boxed sections.

Only full-screen video storytelling. 

Keep in mind, all the focus points of the videos are centered. So fill in with high converting info and facts, and CTA about corn on the sides of the pages. The Navbar should be transparent, becoming into pill form when scrolled down.
